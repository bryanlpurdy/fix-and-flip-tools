# Fix & Flip Tools

A web-based suite of tools for real estate investors and Realtors, built for internal use by [Blue Star Realty Group](https://bluestarrealtygroup.com).

## Tools

### For Real Estate Investors
- **Deal Analyzer** — Analyze acquisition costs, rehab budgets, financing, and expected profit on a fix-and-flip deal.
- **Property Walkthrough** — Mobile-first repair estimator: walk a property, toggle items by category, attach photos, and save a shareable estimate to the cloud.

### For Realtors
- **Seller Net Sheet** — Estimate seller net proceeds at closing. Calculate agent fees, closing costs, prorated taxes, and loan payoff. Shareable link and print/PDF output.
- **Make Ready List** *(Coming Soon)* — Walk a property with sellers before listing. Prioritize items, estimate costs, and share an action plan with your client.
- **Bookkeeping Light** *(Coming Soon)* — Track per-property expenses across photography, marketing, staging, and more.

## Access

Available at [tools.bluestarrealtygroup.com](https://tools.bluestarrealtygroup.com). Sign in once to access all tools and sync your data across devices.
